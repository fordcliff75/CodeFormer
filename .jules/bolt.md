## 2024-04-25 - GPU Tensor Post-Processing Optimization\n**Learning:** In PyTorch codebases, leaving operations like `.round()` and clamping on the CPU during `tensor2img` conversions creates an unnecessary performance bottleneck due to PCIe data transfers of full 32-bit float tensors.\n**Action:** Optimize single-image processing paths by running `.clamp()`, mathematical scaling, and `.round().type(torch.uint8)` on the GPU *before* moving the tensor to the CPU via `.cpu().numpy()`, which reduces PCIe data transfer by 4x and is noticeably faster than the traditional `tensor2img` implementation with `make_grid` loops.
